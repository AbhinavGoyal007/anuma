# Product Vision

## Purpose

ANUMA AI makes important frontline interactions measurable, understandable, and actionable. It fills the information gap between the events an organization already records—lead created, visit completed, quote issued, purchase won or lost—and the human conversation that influenced those events.

The initial category is **Frontline Interaction Intelligence** for high-value, offline customer interactions in multilingual and field-intensive markets. The first pilot packs are automotive retail and electronics retail in India.

## Product thesis

The MVP succeeds if real conversations produce useful management insight that a client cannot reliably obtain today. It must answer:

1. What is the customer trying to achieve?
2. What happened in the interaction?
3. What did the representative do or omit relative to the client's process?
4. What patterns are emerging across customers, products, locations, and representatives?
5. What happened to the opportunity afterward?

Speech-to-text is infrastructure. The transcript is evidence. The product is the reusable, queryable, evidence-backed intelligence derived from the interaction.

## Intelligence layers

### Customer intelligence

Needs, questions, preferences, constraints, price expectations, comparisons, objections, confusion, complaints, unanswered questions, and emerging themes.

### Frontline performance intelligence

Observable behavior such as discovery, product explanation, question answering, objection handling, closing, and next-step discipline. ANUMA evaluates behavior against the client's playbook; it does not infer personality, emotion, or intent.

### Process and opportunity intelligence

Whether relevant commercial activities occurred: budget discovery, finance discussion, demonstration, test drive, warranty explanation, follow-up, and client-defined procedures.

### Outcome intelligence

Event-based outcomes joined to prior observations. Comparisons are descriptive and disclose cohorts and sample size. The product says “booked conversations were more likely to contain…” and never claims observational association proves causation.

## Product principles

- **Evidence before judgment.** Every important claim links to the source conversation and timestamp.
- **Facts before prose.** Store queryable objects first; prose is a presentation layer.
- **Extract once, reuse many times.** Semantic analysis produces reusable observations; aggregate intelligence and client-specific evaluation consume those observations rather than repeatedly interpreting the full transcript.
- **Insight before dashboard volume.** Surface operational findings with cohort, period, sample size, and drill-down.
- **Business language before AI language.** Use Customer Intelligence, Evidence, Questions, Objections, Process, Coaching, and Outcome Intelligence.
- **Human correction is product data.** Preserve both model output and reviewed truth.
- **No false precision.** Label measured, estimated, inferred, uncertain, and unsupported values honestly.
- **Configuration over forks.** Client playbooks and vertical packs configure one neutral platform.
- **Privacy by architecture.** Isolation, consent, access, retention, and auditability are core data concerns.

## Users and jobs

### Representative

Capture an interaction, see processing state, review customer needs and next actions, inspect limited evidence-backed coaching, and revisit their own conversations.

### Manager

Review team interactions, inspect and correct evidence-backed intelligence, understand recurring customer themes and frontline patterns, and enter or confirm outcomes.

### Organization administrator

Manage membership, locations, vertical packs, trackers, scorecards, retention, and organization settings.

### Internal quality workflow

Compare model, prompt, taxonomy, and provider runs against approved synthetic, de-identified, or explicitly authorized fixtures without creating an ordinary internal membership role in customer organizations, exposing cross-tenant data, or collapsing performance into one “AI accuracy” score.

## Success evidence for the pilot

- Processing coverage and failure reasons are visible.
- Managers judge extracted facts, questions, objections, and next steps useful and correctable.
- Every insight can be drilled into source conversations and evidence.
- The system surfaces recurring themes or process gaps previously unavailable to management.
- Outcome labels accumulate from the beginning and later permit adequately sampled descriptive comparisons.
- Corrections and evaluation fixtures reveal where model or taxonomy quality must improve.

## Non-goals

ANUMA is not a meeting assistant, call-center suite, generic transcript summarizer, open-ended chatbot, universal sales coach, employee surveillance tool, or wrapper around a speech/model vendor. It does not perform emotion, personality, age, gender, or causal inference.
